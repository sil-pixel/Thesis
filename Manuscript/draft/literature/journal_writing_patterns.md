# AIIM Writing Conventions

**Status: PROVISIONAL (2026-10-14).** "Common" patterns below are inferred from 5 abstracts only (`aiim_paper_analysis.md`). Full-text study of section structure, notation and figure conventions is pending (TODO L-01). Mandatory items come only from the Guide for Authors PDF.

## Mandatory (explicit in Guide for Authors)
- Numbered sections and subsections (1, 1.1, 1.1.1); abstract unnumbered; cross-reference by number (p.17).
- Abstract ≤ 250 words, stand-alone, no references, abbreviations defined (p.12–13).
- 1–7 keywords (p.13). Highlights optional: 3–5 bullets ≤ 85 characters (p.13).
- Equations as editable text, numbered consecutively, italic variables, `exp`, solidus for small fractions (p.13).
- Tables: no vertical rules or shading, notes below, cited in order (p.13–14).
- Figure captions: brief title + description, all abbreviations explained; separate files (p.14).
- Numbered references in square brackets in order of appearance; >6 authors → 6 + et al.; DOIs (p.18–19).
- Ethics statement with approval date + reference number (p.4, p.10).
- CRediT statement; Declaration of competing interest; Funding; GenAI declaration section before references; Acknowledgements directly before references (p.6–7, p.17).
- Methodological papers must compare with other proposals and **explicitly discuss elements of novelty** (p.2).

## Common (observed in sampled abstracts)
- **Structured abstracts** with labelled parts (Motivation/Methods/Results/Conclusion in Borsos 2024; Background and objective/Methodology/Results/Conclusion in Hancox 2024). Eijpe 2026 and Sheikhalishahi 2023 use unstructured abstracts. → Both formats are acceptable. A structured abstract suits this paper.
- Quantitative results in the abstract with the primary metric and comparator (Borsos: AUC per modality and fused).
- An explicit interpretability-validation sentence when interpretability is claimed (Sheikhalishahi, Eijpe).
- Closing sentence framed as feasibility / support for application, with limited claims of clinical deployment.
