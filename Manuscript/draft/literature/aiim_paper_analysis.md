## Cross-paper observations (abstract-level; to be confirmed with full text)
1. **Claim vs demonstration:** Papers 1–3 claim performance *and* provide a second line of evidence (parameter efficiency; agreement of interpretations with independent methods and clinicians; multi-cohort testing). DCMFNet currently has neither performance superiority nor independent validation of its interpretations.
2. **Interpretability is validated, not asserted** (2, 3). This directly applies to D-12.
3. **Reviews in AIIM emphasise risk of bias** (5). A leakage-controlled benchmark is in scope for that discussion.
4. **Generalizable to DCMFNet:** report parameter count vs n; validate gates against post-hoc attribution; prefer multi-split or external evaluation; make clinical motivation concrete.
5. **Not generalizable:** none of these papers uses population twin-cohort questionnaire data. Their sample-size and outcome conventions (binary AUC) differ from this regression setting.
